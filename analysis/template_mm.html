The MetaMask Chrome build {{VERSION}} came from the MetaMask GitHub releases, checked against the release's SHA256SUMS and its Sigstore provenance. Chromium (Playwright, persistent profile, QUIC off) loaded it unpacked and ran behind mitmproxy, so every request from the extension pages, its service worker, its snaps and the swap page was recorded with headers and bodies. A blank-browser baseline removed Chromium's own traffic by host. {{BASELINE}}

Requests are attributed by their Origin or Referer header: an http(s) site is the dapp page; a chrome-extension:// origin, the "null" origin of MetaMask's sandboxed snaps, or none is MetaMask. Identifiers were found by searching URLs, headers and bodies for accounts A and B, contact R, the hashes of the run's transactions, and the Bitcoin, Solana, Tron and Stellar addresses (and Bitcoin script hashes) derived from the phrase with standard paths. All eight derived addresses appear in the capture. Each request gets the highest tier whose rule it matches:

Limits: one scripted run, fresh profile, Sepolia only. The multisig steps of the Safe journey have no MetaMask equivalent and were not run.
