The signer was an injected EIP-1193 provider whose keys and RPC stay outside the browser. A blank-page baseline run removed the browser's own traffic. Identifiers were found by searching URLs, headers and bodies for the Safe address, the owners, the counterparty, safeTxHash, CoW order ids and analytics client IDs. Cookie banner choice: {{COOKIES}}.
